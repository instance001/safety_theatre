# Submission + PhilArchive / PhilPapers packaging

This repo is set up so you can (a) generate a conventional PDF, and (b) copy/paste
clean metadata into PhilArchive (and other preprint platforms).

## Recommended baseline (PhilArchive-first)

If your goal is PhilPapers visibility for a philosophy-adjacent preprint, a clean
default is:

1. Produce a stable PDF for the main paper (`dist/safety-theatre-agency.pdf`).
2. Upload to PhilArchive (which feeds PhilPapers).
3. Optionally upload the AI spin-off as a separate PhilArchive entry, explicitly marked
   as downstream/companion.

## Cover letter template (journal submission)

Replace bracketed fields as needed.

Dear Editor,

I am submitting the manuscript “Safety Theatre and the Suppression of Agency: How Risk Discourse Becomes a Control Mechanism” for consideration.

The paper examines a structural pattern observable across multiple domains in which
contemporary safety frameworks increasingly prioritize procedural compliance and moral
signaling over the cultivation of judgment and competence. Rather than focusing on
intent or isolated failures, the analysis addresses systemic outcomes—specifically, how
responsibility reallocation and discretion reduction can produce institutional fragility
under novel conditions.

The manuscript is intentionally domain-general and engages with existing literature on
risk management, organizational behavior, and governance (e.g., Scott; Power; Vaughan;
Perrow). It does not argue against regulation or safety per se, but explores how
certain safety mechanisms may inadvertently discourage agency and degrade adaptive
capacity.

I believe the paper may be of interest to readers concerned with the design and
evaluation of safety, risk, and governance systems across sectors.

Thank you for your consideration.

Sincerely,  
Anthony Paterson  
Fractal Media Infrastructure (FMI)  
https://github.com/instance001
